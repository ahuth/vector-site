Privacy and terms

Last updated October 28, 2026

Your code, prompts and conversations stay on your Mac. Vector sends anonymous usage events so we can see what's working, and you can turn that off. The beta is free and provided as is.

Privacy

Your work stays on your Mac

Workspaces, conversations and files are stored on your computer. Vector doesn't have servers that receive them.

Agents connect to their providers directly

Vector runs the Claude and Codex tools you install, signed in with your own accounts. What those agents send to Anthropic and OpenAI is covered by your agreements with them and by their privacy policies, not this one.

Anonymous usage analytics in the app

Vector sends anonymous events through Aptabase, such as the app opening or an agent starting and which provider it uses. Events include the app version, macOS version and your approximate location at the country level.

Vector never sends your code, prompts, agent output, file names, paths or workspace titles. Events aren't linked to an account or to an identifier that follows you over time.

Analytics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Vector's settings.

Update checks

Vector checks GitHub for new versions. Like any download, that request shares your IP address with GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

getvector.sh counts visits with Umami. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. It records pages viewed, the referring site, browser, operating system, device type and country. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

What we do with it

We use analytics only to understand how Vector and this site are used, so we can improve them. We don't sell analytics data or share it for advertising.
