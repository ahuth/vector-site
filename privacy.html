Privacy and terms

Last updated November 24, 2026

Your code, prompts and conversations stay on your Mac. Vector sends anonymous usage events so we can see what's working, and you can turn that off. Vector costs $99 per year for updates, and the app keeps working if you stop paying.

Privacy

Your work stays on your Mac

Workspaces, conversations and files are stored on your computer. Vector doesn't have servers that receive them.

Agents connect to their providers directly

Vector runs the Claude and Codex tools you install, signed in with your own accounts. What those agents send to Anthropic and OpenAI is covered by your agreements with them and by their privacy policies, not this one.

Anonymous usage analytics in the app

Vector sends anonymous events through PostHog, such as the app opening, a conversation starting or a message being sent, and which provider it uses. Events include the app version and macOS version. PostHog sees your IP address when events arrive, but doesn't store it or use it to estimate your location.

Vector never sends your code, prompts, agent output, file names, paths or workspace titles. Events aren't linked to an account. They carry a random ID for your install so we can count installs. Turning analytics off deletes it, and turning them back on creates a new one.

Analytics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Vector's settings.

Update checks

Vector checks GitHub for new versions. Like any download, that request shares your IP address with GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying a license

Vector is sold through Polar, which is the merchant of record. Polar handles checkout and sales tax, and uses Stripe to process payments. There are no Vector accounts.

When you buy, Polar collects your email, name, billing address and payment details. Vector's author can see the customer records Polar keeps, such as your email, so we can contact you about your purchase and updates. We won't send marketing email unless you opt in separately.

The free trial needs no signup. It is tracked only on your Mac, and we never receive anything about it.

License keys

When Vector checks your license key, it sends the key to Polar's API. Like any request, that shares your IP address with Polar, under Polar's privacy policy. Vector stores the key in the macOS keychain. It reads only whether the key is valid and doesn't keep any other details from the response.

Analytics events are never linked to a purchase or a license key. They stay anonymous.

Deleting your purchase data

To ask for your purchase data to be deleted, email hello@getvector.sh. Polar and Stripe may need to keep some records for tax and legal reasons, so we can't always delete everything.

Who handles your data

For purchases, Polar handles checkout, tax and license keys, and Stripe handles payments through Polar. Anonymous analytics in the app go through PostHog. Update checks go to GitHub. This website uses Umami and is hosted on GitHub Pages.

This website

getvector.sh counts visits with Umami. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites. It records pages viewed, the referring site, browser, operating system, device type and country. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

What we do with it

We use analytics only to understand how Vector and this site are used, so we can improve them. We don't sell analytics data or share it for advertising.

We use purchase details only to run your license and to contact you about your purchase and updates.

Terms

Price and trial

Vector costs $99 per year for updates, sold through Polar. Every install gets a 14 day free trial that starts at first launch, with no signup. After the trial, you need a license key from a purchase to keep using Vector.

Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews every year unless you cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the year you've paid for. To cancel, use Polar's customer portal, which you can open from Vector's settings.

If your subscription ends

Vector keeps working at the version you have. Only updates stop. You can renew or buy a new key at any time to get updates again.

Using Vector

You may download and use Vector for your own work, personal or commercial. Please don't redistribute or resell it.

You're in charge of your agents

Agents in Vector can edit files and run commands on your computer. Review what they do, and keep your work in version control.

No warranty

Vector is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent the law allows, we aren't liable for any damages or data loss from using it.

Changes

We may update this page as Vector changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
